# Kestrel Bay Municipal Code (selected sections)

Kestrel Bay is a city that doesn't exist. This code is written for the permit desk lab and has no legal force anywhere.

## KBC 1.08.010 Applications and review times

An application for a business permit is reviewed within ten working days. A complete application that meets every rule that applies is approved. An application that needs a decision the code leaves to a person, such as a limit that could be met in more than one way, is referred to a reviewer.

## KBC 6.02.010 Retail stores: general permit

A retail store needs a general business permit. A store that also sells food prepared on site needs a café permit under section 6.04.020 as well. Hardware stores that sell paint, fuel or other flammable goods must store them in a cabinet approved by the fire department.

## KBC 6.04.020 Cafés and coffee shops

A café or coffee shop needs a café permit. At least one person on every shift must hold a food handler certificate. A café may serve food prepared on site, but not cook with open flame unless the kitchen has a hood approved by the fire department.

## KBC 6.04.050 Outdoor seating on sidewalks

A café may place seats on the sidewalk in front of its own property, up to 8 seats, if a clear path of at least 1.5 metres is left for people walking. More than 8 seats needs a sidewalk use licence, which is decided by a reviewer. Outdoor seats must be brought in by 10 pm.

## KBC 9.22.030 Noise limits after 10 pm

After 10 pm and until 7 am, music and voices from a business may not be heard inside any home nearby. Outdoor speakers are not allowed after 10 pm.

## KBC 18.10.015 Mixed-use zone MU-1: permitted uses

In zone MU-1 (mixed use), these uses are permitted: retail stores, cafés and coffee shops, offices, studios, and homes above the ground floor. Restaurants with more than 30 seats and any business open after 11 pm need a conditional use permit.

## KBC 18.10.040 Mixed-use zone MU-1: deliveries

Deliveries in zone MU-1 may take place between 7 am and 8 pm. Trucks longer than 9 metres must use the loading bay at the rear of the building where one exists.
