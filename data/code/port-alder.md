# Port Alder Municipal Code (selected sections)

Port Alder is a city that doesn't exist. This code is written for the permit desk lab and has no legal force anywhere.

## PAC 1.04.020 Permit renewals

A business permit is valid for one year. The holder may renew it in the thirty days before it ends. A renewal is approved without review when the business type, the address, the zone, the number of staff and the signs are the same as in the permit being renewed. Any change sends the renewal to a full review under the section that applies to the change.

## PAC 5.04.030 Permit fees

The fee for a new business permit is 180 dollars, and 60 dollars for a renewal. Food businesses pay an added inspection fee of 95 dollars. Fees are due when the application is filed and are refunded in full if the application is withdrawn before review.

## PAC 5.04.090 Appeals

An applicant may appeal a denial within fifteen days of the decision. The appeal goes to the Permit Appeals Board, which decides within thirty days. A referral to a reviewer is not a denial and can't be appealed.

## PAC 5.12.010 Food businesses: who needs a permit

Every business that prepares, cooks, bakes or serves food to the public needs a food business permit, in addition to its business permit. This includes bakeries, cafés, restaurants, caterers and mobile food vendors. Selling only food that is sealed and packaged elsewhere does not need a food business permit.

## PAC 5.12.040 Mobile food vendors

A mobile food vendor, such as a food truck or a cart, needs a food business permit and a vending location approval. A vendor may stop in commercial zones for up to four hours at one location each day, and never within 30 metres of a restaurant's entrance. Each vehicle must carry its own food handler certificate.

## PAC 5.12.060 Food handler certificate

At least one person on every shift of a food business must hold a current food handler certificate. The certificate is issued after a course of eight hours and is valid for three years. The applicant must file a copy of the certificate with the application.

## PAC 5.12.080 Catering kitchens

A caterer that prepares food for events must work from an inspected commercial kitchen, its own or a shared one under contract. The application names the kitchen and includes its latest inspection. A caterer serving more than 200 guests at one event must also notify the fire department.

## PAC 5.20.010 Personal care salons

Hair, nail and beauty salons need a personal care permit. Every person who works on clients must hold a state licence for that work. A salon that also sells food or drink prepared on site needs a food business permit as well.

## PAC 9.10.020 Noise in commercial zones

Between 10 pm and 7 am, sound from a business may not exceed 55 decibels at the property line. Deliveries and the use of loud equipment such as mixers and compressors outside are not allowed in those hours.

## PAC 17.20.030 Zone C-2: uses allowed by right

In zone C-2 (neighbourhood commercial), these uses are allowed by right, without a hearing: retail stores, bakeries, cafés, personal care salons, offices and catering kitchens with fewer than 10 staff. A use allowed by right still needs every permit the code requires for that business.

## PAC 17.20.050 Zone C-2: uses that need a conditional permit

In zone C-2, these uses need a conditional use permit after a public hearing: bars, restaurants with more than 40 seats, businesses with 10 or more staff on one shift, and any business open after midnight.

## PAC 17.40.110 Signs in commercial zones

In commercial zones, a business may have one sign facing each street. A sign may be at most 2 square metres. Signs may be lit from outside, but not flash or move. A larger sign needs a variance.
